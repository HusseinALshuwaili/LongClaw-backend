IN ALERTS. WE BUILT
AGENTS THAT DON'T.

SENTINEL_AGENTS started from one observation: most security alerts are noise, but you can't tell which ones until someone — or something — actually reads them.

THE PROBLEM

A mid-size security team sees thousands of alerts a day across EDR, SIEM, email, and cloud config tools. Almost all of it is noise. The signal gets lost, analysts burn out, and the one alert that mattered slips through at 2am.

OUR APPROACH

We don't ask you to trust a black box with full autonomy. Our agents classify and recommend; a human confirms every real action. The agents that run actual scans only touch assets you've explicitly verified — no surprises, no unscoped probing.

/02 WHAT WE WON'T DO
⛔

No silent autonomy

No agent closes a ticket, blocks a sender, or pushes a patch without a human confirming first.

⛔

No unscoped scanning

Tactical agents only touch assets you've verified ownership of — nothing outside the registry, ever.

⛔

No opaque scoring

Every classification ships with a plain-English rationale, not just a number.
