/03 COMMON QUESTIONS
Can agents take action without me?
Not in V1. Every plan is classify-only — agents recommend an action and a confidence score, and a human confirms before anything closes, blocks, or patches.
What can tactical agents actually scan?
Only assets you've explicitly verified via a DNS TXT record or signed cloud IAM role. Each asset has its own scope boundary and rate limit — nothing is probed outside that.
Can I change tiers later?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime; your asset registry and agent history carry over.
Do you offer a trial?
Yes, a 14-day pilot on the Starter tier with up to 10 assets. Reach out via the contact page to set one up.
