Why ship 4, not 12?

Building all 12 agents before validating that anyone wants this is the easiest way to waste months on the wrong thing. Triage, Network Recon, Vuln Scanner, and Config Audit cover the most common, highest-signal entry point — alert fatigue plus exposed infrastructure — with agents that are real, auditable, and scoped to assets you've explicitly verified. If that resonates, the other 8 get built next. If it doesn't, we've learned that cheaply.
